Judge two answers to the same rider's question, each written from the same
search findings.

1. **Faithfulness**: every statement is supported by the source it cites;
   nothing is added that the findings do not say.
2. **Directness**: the rider can tell the answer to each part of their
   question at a glance.
3. **Clarity**: plain words, no repetition, sources easy to follow.

Whether every claim carries a citation, and whether the required facts are
stated, is measured separately by the required check; do not re-grade it here.
