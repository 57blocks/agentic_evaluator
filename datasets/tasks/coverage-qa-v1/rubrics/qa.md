# Coverage answers

Whether the yes / no / not_addressed answer is right, and whether the
deciding sections are cited, is already graded by checks/qa-check.mjs.
Judge only the `explanation`, read beside the policy and the question.

## faithful-explanation

Does the explanation follow from the cited clauses, and only from them?

- Every condition it states is in the policy; nothing is added from general
  knowledge of what plans usually cover.
- When two clauses decide it together (a criterion and its definition, a
  rule and its exception), it says how they combine.
- When the revision history changed a rule, it applies the current rule.
- For not_addressed, it says the policy is silent or out of scope, and does
  not guess what the answer would be.

## caller-ready

Could a member services agent read it to the caller as is? Plain words, the
deciding fact first (the number, the date, the condition), no hedging the
policy does not call for. Shorter is better when nothing is missing.
