What this evaluates

Which model answers a coverage question from a published medical policy correctly and cites the clause that decides it? 30 questions on three synthetic policies; 21 hard (two clauses together, exceptions, a revised threshold, scope, limits, and silent policies). Graded by script on the answer and citations; the explanation is judged, pairwise as well, so a person's blind review calibrates the judge.

A pass of qa-check means: The answer (yes, no, not_addressed) matches the key, every deciding section is cited, and no cited section is missing from the policy; the explanation is left to the judge.
