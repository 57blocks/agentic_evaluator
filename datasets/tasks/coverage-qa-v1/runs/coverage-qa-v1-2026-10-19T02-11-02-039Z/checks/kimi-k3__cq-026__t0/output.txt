  "explanation": "Yes. Telehealth PT visits are covered on the same terms as in-person visits, and the only telehealth-specific requirement is that the initial evaluation be in person—which the member has already completed. Follow-up telehealth visits still count toward the combined 30-visit-per-calendar-year PT/OT limit and must be with a licensed physical therapist or supervised PT assistant."
