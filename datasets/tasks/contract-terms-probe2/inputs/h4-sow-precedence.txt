MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of July 14, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Granite Peak Outdoor Co., a Colorado corporation ("Client"), and Sablewood Digital, Inc., a California corporation ("Vendor").

1. DEFINITIONS. "Deliverables" means the materials Vendor delivers under an SOW. "SOW" means a statement of work signed by both parties that references this Agreement.

2. SERVICES. Vendor shall perform the services described in each SOW. Each SOW forms part of this Agreement.

3. CHANGE ORDERS. Either party may request a change to an SOW. No change is binding until a written change order is signed by both parties.

4. PERSONNEL. Vendor shall assign qualified personnel and may replace key personnel only with Client's consent, which Client shall not unreasonably withhold.

5. FEES AND EXPENSES. Client shall pay the fees in each SOW within thirty (30) days of invoice. Pre-approved travel expenses are reimbursed at cost.

6. ACCEPTANCE. Client has ten (10) business days to accept or reject each Deliverable. Silence is acceptance.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with at least the care it uses for its own, and no less than reasonable care, for three (3) years after disclosure.

8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. On payment, Client owns the Deliverables. Vendor keeps its pre-existing materials and grants Client a perpetual license to use any of them incorporated in a Deliverable.

9. WARRANTIES. Vendor warrants that the services will be performed in a professional manner consistent with industry standards. Client's exclusive remedy for breach of this warranty is re-performance.

10. INDEMNIFICATION. Vendor shall defend Client against third-party claims that a Deliverable infringes a United States patent, copyright or trademark.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY. Except for Section 10, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Client under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

12. INSURANCE. Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and cyber liability insurance of at least $2,000,000.

13. TERM AND TERMINATION.
13.1 This Agreement has an initial term of two (2) years from the Effective Date.
13.2 This Agreement then renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.
13.3 Either party may terminate an SOW for convenience on sixty (60) days' written notice.

14. NON-SOLICITATION. During the term and for twelve (12) months after, neither party shall solicit the other's employees who worked on an SOW.

15. ORDER OF PRECEDENCE. If this Agreement conflicts with an SOW, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies the section of this Agreement it overrides by section number.

16. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement and every SOW are governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

17. GENERAL. Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except in a merger or sale of substantially all assets. Notices must be in writing. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts.
