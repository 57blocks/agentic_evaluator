LABORATORY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Laboratory Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by Ashgrove Biologics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Sponsor"), and Pellucid Lab Services LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("Lab"), as of the Effective Date.

ARTICLE 1 — DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Effective Date" means January 1, 2025.
1.2 "Initial Term" has the meaning given in Section 9.1.
1.3 "Lookback Period" means the eighteen (18) month period immediately preceding the event giving rise to a claim.
1.4 "Notice Period" means seventy-five (75) days.
1.5 "Renewal Term" means a period of twenty-four (24) months.
1.6 "Services" means the assay development and sample testing services described in each Work Order.
1.7 "Work Order" means a written order for Services signed by both parties.

ARTICLE 2 — SERVICES
2.1 Lab shall perform the Services in accordance with each Work Order, applicable good laboratory practice and the specifications in the Work Order.
2.2 Lab shall retain samples for ninety (90) days after reporting results, unless a Work Order states otherwise.

ARTICLE 3 — FEES
3.1 Sponsor shall pay the fees set out in each Work Order within forty-five (45) days of invoice.

ARTICLE 9 — TERM
9.1 The initial term of this Agreement (the "Initial Term") begins on the Effective Date and ends on December 31, 2027.
9.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive Renewal Terms unless either party notifies the other in writing of non-renewal no later than the Notice Period before the end of the then-current term.
9.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement on ninety (90) days' notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure within thirty (30) days.

ARTICLE 12 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
12.1 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.
12.2 Each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Sponsor to Lab during the Lookback Period (the "Liability Cap").

ARTICLE 20 — GOVERNING LAW
20.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
20.2 The parties submit to the jurisdiction of the courts of Suffolk County, Massachusetts.
