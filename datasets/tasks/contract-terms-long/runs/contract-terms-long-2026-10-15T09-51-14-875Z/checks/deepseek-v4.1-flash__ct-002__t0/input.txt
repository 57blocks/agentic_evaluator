1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.3 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Contractor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.4 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.

2.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set out on the signature page or as later updated by notice.
