What this evaluates

Does contract-term extraction hold up on long, scanned document packets? Six synthetic packets of about 9,000 words (an agreement with the graded articles in its back half, five schedules, six SOWs with their own terms, caps and laws as distractors) at three scan-noise levels; character damage never touches a graded value. Extract eight terms as they stand on the status date.

A pass of terms-check means: All eight extracted fields match the key the generator computed (amendments applied by effective date and signature).
