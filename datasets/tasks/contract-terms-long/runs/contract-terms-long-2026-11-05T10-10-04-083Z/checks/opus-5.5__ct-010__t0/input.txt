MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of October 16, 2022 (the "Effective Date") by and between Brookhaven Logistics Holdings, Inc., a Minnesota corporation ("Contractor"), and Halvorsen Outdoor Inc., a California corporation ("Company").

RECITALS. Company wishes to obtain, and Contractor wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Dallas are authorized to close.
1.3 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.4 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Contractor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.

SECTION 2. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND AMENDMENT
2.1 This Agreement, including its Schedules and each SOW, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.
2.2 This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

SECTION 3. CHANGE CONTROL
3.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Contractor shall respond within 5 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
3.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Contractor is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 4. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTING
4.1 Contractor shall assign Personnel with the skills and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Contractor may replace key Personnel named in an SOW only with Company's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
4.2 Contractor may use subcontractors only with Company's prior written consent. Contractor remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.
4.3 Contractor shall ensure that its Personnel comply with Company's reasonable site, security and safety rules when on Company's premises.

SECTION 5. TAXES
5.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Company shall pay any such taxes that Contractor is required to collect, other than taxes on Contractor's net income, property or employees.
5.2 If Company is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Contractor with evidence of payment within 180 days.

SECTION 6. EXPENSES
6.1 Company shall reimburse Contractor for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Company has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Company's travel policy as provided to Contractor.
6.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 90 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 7. FEES AND INVOICING
7.1 Company shall pay all undisputed amounts within 60 days after the date of Contractor's invoice. Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears unless an SOW states otherwise.
7.2 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.
7.3 Company may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Contractor of the dispute within 30 days of the invoice date, with reasonable detail of the reasons. The parties shall work together to resolve any dispute promptly.

SECTION 8. CONFIDENTIALITY
8.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
8.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Personnel and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section.
8.3 These obligations continue for three (3) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.
8.4 Within 120 days after a written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or as required by law.

SECTION 9. DISPUTE ESCALATION
9.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through their project managers. If unresolved within 60 days, either party may escalate it to an executive of each party, who shall meet within a further 90 days.
9.2 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

SECTION 10. INSURANCE
10.1 Contractor shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better, commercial general liability insurance of at least five million dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, professional liability insurance of at least two million dollars ($2,000,000) per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.
10.2 Contractor shall provide certificates of insurance on request and give Company at least 15 days' notice before any required policy is cancelled or materially reduced.

SECTION 11. STATEMENTS OF WORK
11.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.
11.2 Contractor shall not begin work under an SOW until it has been signed by both parties. Work performed before signature is at Contractor's own risk and expense.
11.3 Each SOW will identify a project manager for each party. The project managers will meet at least every 30 days to review progress, risks and open decisions.
11.4 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies by section number the provision of this Agreement that it overrides.

SECTION 12. FORCE MAJEURE
12.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
12.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 180 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 13. SEVERABILITY AND WAIVER
13.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it.

SECTION 14. COUNTERPARTS

SECTION 15. DATA PROTECTION

SECTION 18. TERMINATION
