RECITALS. Company wishes to obtain, and Contractor wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Boston are authorized to close.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.4 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Contractor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.

SECTION 2. INDEMNIFICATION
2.1 Contractor shall defend Company against any third-party claim alleging that a deliverable infringes a patent, copyright or trademark, and pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
2.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party to control its defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

SECTION 3. VENUE
3.1 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Franklin County, Ohio, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

SECTION 4. PUBLICITY
4.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 15 days after receiving written notice describing it.
5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
5.3 Company may terminate any SOW for convenience on 20 days' written notice, paying for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

SECTION 6. EFFECT OF TERMINATION
6.1 On termination, Company shall pay all fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other's Confidential Information as provided in this Agreement.
6.2 At Company's request, Contractor shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to 15 days after termination, at the rates then in effect.

SECTION 7. TAXES
7.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Company shall pay any such taxes that Contractor is required to collect, other than taxes on Contractor's net income, property or employees.
7.2 If Company is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Contractor with evidence of payment within 45 days.

SECTION 8. DATA PROTECTION
8.1 To the extent Contractor processes personal data on behalf of Company, it shall do so only on Company's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.

8.3 Contractor shall retain Company's data for no longer than 180 days after the end of the applicable SOW, after which it shall securely delete that data unless retention is required by law.
