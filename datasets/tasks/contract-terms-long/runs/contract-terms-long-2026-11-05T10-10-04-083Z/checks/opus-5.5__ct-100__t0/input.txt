TECHNOLOGY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Technology Services Agreement (this "Agreement") takes effect on the date on which it has been signed by both parties (the "Effective Date") by and between Quillstone Biologics Corp., a California corporation ("Supplier"), and Larkspur Software Corp., a Ohio corporation ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Dallas are authorized to close.
1.3 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.4 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. FORCE MAJEURE
2.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
2.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 30 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 3. STATEMENTS OF WORK
3.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.
3.2 Supplier shall not begin work under an SOW until it has been signed by both parties. Work performed before signature is at Supplier's own risk and expense.
3.3 Each SOW will identify a project manager for each party. The project managers will meet at least every 14 days to review progress, risks and open decisions.
3.4 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies by section number the provision of this Agreement that it overrides.

SECTION 4. ASSIGNMENT
4.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 5. COUNTERPARTS
5.1 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

SECTION 6. INDEMNIFICATION
6.1 Supplier shall defend Buyer against any third-party claim alleging that a deliverable infringes a patent, copyright or trademark, and pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
6.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party to control its defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

SECTION 7. TAXES
7.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Buyer shall pay any such taxes that Supplier is required to collect, other than taxes on Supplier's net income, property or employees.
7.2 If Buyer is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Supplier with evidence of payment within 15 days.

SECTION 8. EXPENSES
8.1 Buyer shall reimburse Supplier for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Buyer has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Buyer's travel policy as provided to Supplier.
8.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 120 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 9. INFORMATION SECURITY
9.1 Supplier shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Buyer's data, consistent with a recognized industry framework such as ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
9.2 Supplier shall perform vulnerability scans of systems that process Buyer's data at least every 45 days and remediate critical findings within 45 days.

SECTION 10. DISPUTE ESCALATION
10.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through their project managers. If unresolved within 120 days, either party may escalate it to an executive of each party, who shall meet within a further 30 days.
10.2 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

SECTION 11. PUBLICITY
11.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 12. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS

SECTION 13. NON-SOLICITATION
