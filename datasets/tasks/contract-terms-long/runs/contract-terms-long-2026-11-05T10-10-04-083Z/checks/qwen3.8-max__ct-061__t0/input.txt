RECITALS. Client wishes to obtain, and Vendor wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Vendor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Boston are authorized to close.
1.4 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.

SECTION 2. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
2.1 Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement, including anti-bribery laws and the export control and economic sanctions laws of the United States.
2.2 Neither party shall export, re-export or transfer any deliverable in violation of the export control laws of the United States or of any other applicable jurisdiction.

SECTION 3. ACCEPTANCE
3.1 Client shall test each deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the applicable SOW within 15 Business Days of delivery and notify Vendor in writing of acceptance or of any non-conformity.
3.2 If Client reports a non-conformity, Vendor shall correct it and redeliver within 90 days, and the acceptance procedure shall be repeated. If a deliverable fails acceptance three (3) times, Client may terminate the applicable SOW and recover the fees paid for that deliverable.

SECTION 4. EXPENSES
4.1 Client shall reimburse Vendor for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Client has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Client's travel policy as provided to Vendor.
4.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 120 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 5. VENUE
5.1 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Miami-Dade County, Florida, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

SECTION 6. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
6.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

SECTION 7. ASSIGNMENT
7.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 11. DATA PROTECTION
