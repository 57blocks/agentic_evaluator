MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between Granite Peak Manufacturing LLP, a Virginia limited liability partnership ("Supplier"), and Cobalt Ridge Foods LLC, a New York limited liability company ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.2 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Seattle are authorized to close.
1.4 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.5 "Effective Date" means November 5, 2023.

SECTION 2. PUBLICITY
2.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 3. CHANGE CONTROL
3.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Supplier shall respond within 5 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
3.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Supplier is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 4. INSURANCE
4.1 Supplier shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better, commercial general liability insurance of at least two million dollars ($2,000,000) per occurrence, professional liability insurance of at least three million dollars ($3,000,000) per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.
4.2 Supplier shall provide certificates of insurance on request and give Buyer at least 30 days' notice before any required policy is cancelled or materially reduced.

SECTION 5. ASSIGNMENT
5.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 6. AUDIT
6.1 Buyer may, on 90 days' written notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, audit Supplier's records relating to fees charged under this Agreement.
6.2 Supplier shall keep those records for at least three (3) years after the fees are invoiced. If an audit shows an overcharge of more than five percent (5%), Supplier shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

SECTION 7. SEVERABILITY AND WAIVER
7.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it.

SECTION 8. NON-SOLICITATION
8.1 During the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months after it ends, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was directly involved in performing this Agreement, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

SECTION 9. NOTICES
9.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set out on the signature page or as later updated by notice.
9.2 A notice is effective on receipt, or if delivered by courier, 1 Business Day(s) after dispatch.

SECTION 11. EXPENSES

SECTION 13. CONFIDENTIALITY
13.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
