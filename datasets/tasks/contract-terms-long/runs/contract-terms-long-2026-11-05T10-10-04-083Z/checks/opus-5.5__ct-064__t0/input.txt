1.2 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Vendor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Denver are authorized to close.
1.4 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. INFORMATION SECURITY
2.1 Vendor shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Client's data, consistent with a recognized industry framework such as ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
2.2 Vendor shall perform vulnerability scans of systems that process Client's data at least every 120 days and remediate critical findings within 90 days.

SECTION 3. INSURANCE
3.1 Vendor shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better, commercial general liability insurance of at least five million dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, professional liability insurance of at least three million dollars ($3,000,000) per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.

SECTION 5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.
5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
