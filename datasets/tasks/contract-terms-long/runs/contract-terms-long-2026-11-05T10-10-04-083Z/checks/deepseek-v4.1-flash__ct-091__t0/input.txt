MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") takes effect on the date on which it has been signed by both parties (the "Effective Date") by and between Granite Peak Energy Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and Lumenfield Outdoor Inc., a New York corporation ("Customer").

RECITALS. Customer wishes to obtain, and Provider wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Seattle are authorized to close.
1.4 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Provider makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.

SECTION 2. CONFIDENTIALITY
2.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
2.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Personnel and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section.
2.3 These obligations continue for three (3) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.
2.4 Within 180 days after a written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or as required by law.

SECTION 3. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
3.1 Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement, including anti-bribery laws and the export control and economic sanctions laws of the United States.
3.2 Neither party shall export, re-export or transfer any deliverable in violation of the export control laws of the United States or of any other applicable jurisdiction.

SECTION 4. TAXES
4.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Customer shall pay any such taxes that Provider is required to collect, other than taxes on Provider's net income, property or employees.
4.2 If Customer is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Provider with evidence of payment within 60 days.

SECTION 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 Upon payment in full of the applicable fees, Customer shall own all deliverables created specifically for Customer under an SOW, and Provider hereby assigns to Customer all rights in them.
5.2 Provider retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how, and in any general improvements to them. To the extent any of them is incorporated in a deliverable, Provider grants Customer a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use it as part of that deliverable.

SECTION 6. CHANGE CONTROL
6.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Provider shall respond within 10 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
6.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Provider is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 7. EXPENSES
7.1 Customer shall reimburse Provider for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Customer has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Customer's travel policy as provided to Provider.
7.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 20 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 8. ACCEPTANCE
8.1 Customer shall test each deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the applicable SOW within 20 Business Days of delivery and notify Provider in writing of acceptance or of any non-conformity.
8.2 If Customer reports a non-conformity, Provider shall correct it and redeliver within 90 days, and the acceptance procedure shall be repeated. If a deliverable fails acceptance three (3) times, Customer may terminate the applicable SOW and recover the fees paid for that deliverable.

SECTION 9. VENUE
9.1 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

SECTION 10. PUBLICITY
10.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 11. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTING
11.1 Provider shall assign Personnel with the skills and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Provider may replace key Personnel named in an SOW only with Customer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
11.2 Provider may use subcontractors only with Customer's prior written consent. Provider remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.
11.3 Provider shall ensure that its Personnel comply with Customer's reasonable site, security and safety rules when on Customer's premises.

SECTION 12. INDEMNIFICATION
12.1 Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that a deliverable infringes a patent, copyright or trademark, and pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
12.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party to control its defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

SECTION 13. INSURANCE
13.1 Provider shall maintain, with insurers rated A- or better, commercial general liability insurance of at least five million dollars ($5,000,000) per occurrence, professional liability insurance of at least three million dollars ($3,000,000) per claim, and workers' compensation insurance as required by law.
13.2 Provider shall provide certificates of insurance on request and give Customer at least 30 days' notice before any required policy is cancelled or materially reduced.

SECTION 14. WARRANTIES
14.1 Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that each deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications for 15 days after acceptance.
14.2 Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in this Section is for Provider to re-perform the non-conforming Services or, if Provider cannot do so within a reasonable time, a refund of the fees paid for them.
14.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 15. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
15.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

SECTION 16. ASSIGNMENT
16.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 17. FORCE MAJEURE
17.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
17.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 60 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 18. TERMINATION
18.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 45 days after receiving written notice describing it.
18.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
18.3 Customer may terminate any SOW for convenience on 180 days' written notice, paying for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

SECTION 19. TERM AND RENEWAL
19.1 The initial term of this Agreement is sixty (60) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
19.2 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months each unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

SECTION 20. FEES AND INVOICING
20.1 Customer shall pay all undisputed amounts within 30 days after the date of Provider's invoice. Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears unless an SOW states otherwise.
20.2 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.
20.3 Customer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Provider of the dispute within 120 days of the invoice date, with reasonable detail of the reasons. The parties shall work together to resolve any dispute promptly.

SECTION 21. SEVERABILITY AND WAIVER
21.1 If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid, the remaining provisions remain in effect. A failure to enforce a provision is not a waiver of it.

SECTION 22. DISPUTE ESCALATION
22.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through their project managers. If unresolved within 20 days, either party may escalate it to an executive of each party, who shall meet within a further 15 days.
22.2 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

SECTION 23. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
23.1 Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages, or for lost profits, even if advised of their possibility.
23.2 Except for the excluded claims below, each party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) one million dollars ($1,000,000) and (b) the fees paid or payable by Customer under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
23.3 The limitation above does not apply to a party's indemnification obligations or to its breach of the confidentiality obligations in this Agreement, for which that party's aggregate liability shall not exceed ten million dollars ($10,000,000).

SECTION 24. COUNTERPARTS
24.1 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

SECTION 25. NOTICES
25.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set out on the signature page or as later updated by notice.

26.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising out of it are governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

SECTION 27. STATEMENTS OF WORK
27.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.
27.2 Provider shall not begin work under an SOW until it has been signed by both parties. Work performed before signature is at Provider's own risk and expense.

SECTION 28. EFFECT OF TERMINATION
28.1 On termination, Customer shall pay all fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other's Confidential Information as provided in this Agreement.
