RECITALS. Customer wishes to obtain, and Provider wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Provider makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Dallas are authorized to close.
1.4 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. WARRANTIES
2.1 Provider warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that each deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications for 90 days after acceptance.
2.2 Customer's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in this Section is for Provider to re-perform the non-conforming Services or, if Provider cannot do so within a reasonable time, a refund of the fees paid for them.
2.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 3. FORCE MAJEURE
3.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
3.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 90 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 4. CHANGE CONTROL
4.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Provider shall respond within 10 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
4.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Provider is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 5. NON-SOLICITATION
5.1 During the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months after it ends, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was directly involved in performing this Agreement, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

SECTION 6. INDEMNIFICATION

10.3 Each SOW will identify a project manager for each party. The project managers will meet at least every 7 days to review progress, risks and open decisions.
