MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between Harborview Digital Inc., a Virginia corporation ("Supplier"), and Halvorsen Supply Corp., a California corporation ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Non-Renewal Notice Period" means sixty (60) days.
1.2 "Renewal Term" means a period of twelve (12) months.
1.3 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.4 "Effective Date" means May 28, 2023.
1.5 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.6 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Boston are authorized to close.
1.7 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. FORCE MAJEURE
2.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
2.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 30 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 3. STATEMENTS OF WORK
3.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.
3.2 Supplier shall not begin work under an SOW until it has been signed by both parties. Work performed before signature is at Supplier's own risk and expense.
3.3 Each SOW will identify a project manager for each party. The project managers will meet at least every 14 days to review progress, risks and open decisions.
3.4 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies by section number the provision of this Agreement that it overrides.

SECTION 4. DISPUTE ESCALATION
4.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through their project managers. If unresolved within 15 days, either party may escalate it to an executive of each party, who shall meet within a further 60 days.
4.2 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

SECTION 5. EFFECT OF TERMINATION
5.1 On termination, Buyer shall pay all fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other's Confidential Information as provided in this Agreement.
5.2 At Buyer's request, Supplier shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to 120 days after termination, at the rates then in effect.

SECTION 6. TAXES
6.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Buyer shall pay any such taxes that Supplier is required to collect, other than taxes on Supplier's net income, property or employees.
6.2 If Buyer is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Supplier with evidence of payment within 90 days.

SECTION 7. EXPENSES
7.1 Buyer shall reimburse Supplier for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Buyer has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Buyer's travel policy as provided to Supplier.
7.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 8. NON-SOLICITATION
8.1 During the term of this Agreement and for six (6) months after it ends, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was directly involved in performing this Agreement, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

SECTION 9. INDEMNIFICATION
