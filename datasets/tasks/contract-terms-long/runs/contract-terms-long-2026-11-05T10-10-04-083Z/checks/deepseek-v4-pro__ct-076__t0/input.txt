MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed Services Agreement (this "Agreement") takes effect on the date on which it has been signed by both parties (the "Effective Date") by and between Delmont Media LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Supplier"), and Stonegate Health Partners LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Minneapolis are authorized to close.
1.4 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. COUNTERPARTS
2.1 This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

SECTION 3. PUBLICITY
3.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 4. DATA PROTECTION
4.1 To the extent Supplier processes personal data on behalf of Buyer, it shall do so only on Buyer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.
4.2 Supplier shall notify Buyer without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Buyer's personal data.
4.3 Supplier shall retain Buyer's data for no longer than 120 days after the end of the applicable SOW, after which it shall securely delete that data unless retention is required by law.

SECTION 5. ASSIGNMENT
5.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 6. VENUE
6.1 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Cook County, Illinois, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

SECTION 7. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTING
7.1 Supplier shall assign Personnel with the skills and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Supplier may replace key Personnel named in an SOW only with Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

7.3 Supplier shall ensure that its Personnel comply with Buyer's reasonable site, security and safety rules when on Buyer's premises.

SECTION 8. EXPENSES

SECTION 10. AUDIT
10.1 Buyer may, on 180 days' written notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, audit Supplier's records relating to fees charged under this Agreement.
10.2 Supplier shall keep those records for at least five (5) years after the fees are invoiced. If an audit shows an overcharge of more than five percent (5%), Supplier shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

SECTION 11. COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
11.1 Each party shall comply with all laws applicable to its performance of this Agreement, including anti-bribery laws and the export control and economic sanctions laws of the United States.
