1.2 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Vendor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.3 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.4 "Renewal Term" means a period of twenty-four (24) months.
1.5 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Denver are authorized to close.
1.6 "Non-Renewal Notice Period" means thirty (30) days.

SECTION 2. CONFIDENTIALITY
2.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
