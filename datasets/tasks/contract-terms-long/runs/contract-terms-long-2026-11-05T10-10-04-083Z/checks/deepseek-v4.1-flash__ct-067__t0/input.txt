MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of September 21, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between Lumenfield Supply LLC, a Illinois limited liability company ("Supplier"), and Tidewater Freight Holdings, Inc., a Massachusetts corporation ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Atlanta are authorized to close.
1.4 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.

SECTION 2. CHANGE CONTROL
2.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Supplier shall respond within 10 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
2.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Supplier is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 3. FORCE MAJEURE
3.1 Neither party is liable for any delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including fire, flood, war, epidemic, governmental action or failure of public utilities, provided it gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to resume performance.
3.2 If a force majeure event prevents performance for more than 30 consecutive days, either party may terminate the affected SOW on written notice.

SECTION 4. FEES AND INVOICING
4.1 Buyer shall pay all undisputed amounts within 60 days after the date of Supplier's invoice. Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears unless an SOW states otherwise.
4.2 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.
4.3 Buyer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier of the dispute within 20 days of the invoice date, with reasonable detail of the reasons. The parties shall work together to resolve any dispute promptly.

SECTION 5. INFORMATION SECURITY
5.1 Supplier shall maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards designed to protect Buyer's data, consistent with a recognized industry framework such as ISO/IEC 27001 or the NIST Cybersecurity Framework.
5.2 Supplier shall perform vulnerability scans of systems that process Buyer's data at least every 60 days and remediate critical findings within 120 days.

SECTION 6. STATEMENTS OF WORK
6.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.
6.2 Supplier shall not begin work under an SOW until it has been signed by both parties. Work performed before signature is at Supplier's own risk and expense.
6.3 Each SOW will identify a project manager for each party. The project managers will meet at least every 7 days to review progress, risks and open decisions.
6.4 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies by section number the provision of this Agreement that it overrides.

SECTION 7. AUDIT
7.1 Buyer may, on 15 days' written notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, audit Supplier's records relating to fees charged under this Agreement.
7.2 Supplier shall keep those records for at least five (5) years after the fees are invoiced. If an audit shows an overcharge of more than five percent (5%), Supplier shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

SECTION 8. INDEMNIFICATION
8.1 Supplier shall defend Buyer against any third-party claim alleging that a deliverable infringes a patent, copyright or trademark, and pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
8.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party to control its defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

SECTION 9. WARRANTIES
9.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that each deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications for 10 days after acceptance.
9.2 Buyer's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in this Section is for Supplier to re-perform the non-conforming Services or, if Supplier cannot do so within a reasonable time, a refund of the fees paid for them.
9.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 10. DATA PROTECTION
10.1 To the extent Supplier processes personal data on behalf of Buyer, it shall do so only on Buyer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.
10.2 Supplier shall notify Buyer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Buyer's personal data.
10.3 Supplier shall retain Buyer's data for no longer than 120 days after the end of the applicable SOW, after which it shall securely delete that data unless retention is required by law.

SECTION 11. PUBLICITY
11.1 Neither party shall use the other's name or logo in any publicity, customer list or marketing material without the other's prior written consent.

SECTION 12. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTING
12.1 Supplier shall assign Personnel with the skills and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Supplier may replace key Personnel named in an SOW only with Buyer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
12.2 Supplier may use subcontractors only with Buyer's prior written consent. Supplier remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.
12.3 Supplier shall ensure that its Personnel comply with Buyer's reasonable site, security and safety rules when on Buyer's premises.

SECTION 13. TERMINATION
13.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 15 days after receiving written notice describing it.
13.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
13.3 Buyer may terminate any SOW for convenience on 15 days' written notice, paying for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

SECTION 16. EFFECT OF TERMINATION

16.2 At Buyer's request, Supplier shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to 120 days after termination, at the rates then in effect.

SECTION 18. CONFIDENTIALITY
18.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.

19.1 The initial term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on September 20, 2027 (the "Initial Term").
