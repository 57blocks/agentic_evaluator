SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Effective Date" means March 15, 2025.
1.4 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Atlanta are authorized to close.
1.5 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.

SECTION 2. TERMINATION
2.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 45 days after receiving written notice describing it.
2.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
2.3 Buyer may terminate any SOW for convenience on 120 days' written notice, paying for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

SECTION 3. DATA PROTECTION

SECTION 4. FEES AND INVOICING
4.1 Buyer shall pay all undisputed amounts within 180 days after the date of Supplier's invoice. Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears unless an SOW states otherwise.
4.2 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.
4.3 Buyer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier of the dispute within 90 days of the invoice date, with reasonable detail of the reasons. The parties shall work together to resolve any dispute promptly.

SECTION 5. INFORMATION SECURITY
