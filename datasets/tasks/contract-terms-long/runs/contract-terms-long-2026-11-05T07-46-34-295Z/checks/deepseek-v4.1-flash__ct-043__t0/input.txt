RECITALS. Customer wishes to obtain, and Provider wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Provider makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.4 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Minneapolis are authorized to close.

SECTION 2. TAXES

3.1 To the extent Provider processes personal data on behalf of Customer, it shall do so only on Customer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.

SECTION 4. NON-SOLICITATION
4.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after it ends, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party who was directly involved in performing this Agreement, except through general advertisements not targeted at such employees.

SECTION 5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.
