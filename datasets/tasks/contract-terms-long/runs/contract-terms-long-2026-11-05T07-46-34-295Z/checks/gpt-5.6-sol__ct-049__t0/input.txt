This Technology Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of July 21, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between Ashgrove Supply LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("Consultant"), and Silverbirch Health Partners Holdings, Inc., a Georgia corporation ("Client").

RECITALS. Client wishes to obtain, and Consultant wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Boston are authorized to close.
1.3 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Consultant makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.4 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.

SECTION 2. CHANGE CONTROL
2.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Consultant shall respond within 5 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
2.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Consultant is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 3. PERSONNEL AND SUBCONTRACTING
3.1 Consultant shall assign Personnel with the skills and experience reasonably required to perform the Services. Consultant may replace key Personnel named in an SOW only with Client's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.
3.2 Consultant may use subcontractors only with Client's prior written consent. Consultant remains responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors as if they were its own.
3.3 Consultant shall ensure that its Personnel comply with Client's reasonable site, security and safety rules when on Client's premises.

SECTION 4. ASSIGNMENT
4.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an Affiliate or to a successor to all or substantially all of its business to which this Agreement relates, on written notice to the other party.

SECTION 5. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND AMENDMENT
5.1 This Agreement, including its Schedules and each SOW, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.
5.2 This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.

SECTION 6. INDEMNIFICATION
6.1 Consultant shall defend Client against any third-party claim alleging that a deliverable infringes a patent, copyright or trademark, and pay any damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement.
6.2 The indemnified party shall give prompt notice of the claim, allow the indemnifying party to control its defense, and provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense.

SECTION 7. STATEMENTS OF WORK
7.1 The specific services, deliverables, milestones and fees for each engagement will be set out in a statement of work signed by authorized representatives of both parties (each, an "SOW"). Each SOW incorporates the terms of this Agreement.

7.4 If an SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, unless the SOW expressly identifies by section number the provision of this Agreement that it overrides.
