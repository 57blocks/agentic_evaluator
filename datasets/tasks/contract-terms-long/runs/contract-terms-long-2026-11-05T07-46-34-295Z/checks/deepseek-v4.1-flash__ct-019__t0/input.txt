1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Atlanta are authorized to close.

4.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 45 days after receiving written notice describing it.
