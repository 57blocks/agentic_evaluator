MANAGED SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of August 2, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by and between Brightwater Energy Holdings, Inc., a Oregon corporation ("Vendor"), and Ironleaf Freight Co., a Florida corporation ("Client").

RECITALS. Client wishes to obtain, and Vendor wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Vendor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.2 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.3 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Atlanta are authorized to close.
1.4 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.

SECTION 2. DATA PROTECTION
2.1 To the extent Vendor processes personal data on behalf of Client, it shall do so only on Client's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.
2.2 Vendor shall notify Client without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Client's personal data.
2.3 Vendor shall retain Client's data for no longer than 30 days after the end of the applicable SOW, after which it shall securely delete that data unless retention is required by law.

SECTION 3. CONFIDENTIALITY
3.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
3.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Personnel and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section.
3.3 These obligations continue for two (2) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.
3.4 Within 10 days after a written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or as required by law.

SECTION 4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
4.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

SECTION 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
5.1 Upon payment in full of the applicable fees, Client shall own all deliverables created specifically for Client under an SOW, and Vendor hereby assigns to Client all rights in them.
5.2 Vendor retains all rights in its pre-existing materials, tools and know-how, and in any general improvements to them. To the extent any of them is incorporated in a deliverable, Vendor grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use it as part of that deliverable.

6.1 This Agreement, including its Schedules and each SOW, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.
6.2 This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
