MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between Lumenfield Home Goods Inc., a Minnesota corporation ("Supplier"), and Stonegate Logistics Corp., a Georgia corporation ("Buyer").

RECITALS. Buyer wishes to obtain, and Supplier wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Effective Date" means January 3, 2023.
1.2 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Seattle are authorized to close.
1.3 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.4 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Supplier makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.5 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.

SECTION 2. CHANGE CONTROL
2.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Supplier shall respond within 5 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.
2.2 No change is binding until a change order describing it is signed by both parties. Supplier is not obliged to perform any change until that change order is signed.

SECTION 3. AUDIT
3.1 Buyer may, on 30 days' written notice and no more than once in any twelve (12) month period, audit Supplier's records relating to fees charged under this Agreement.
3.2 Supplier shall keep those records for at least three (3) years after the fees are invoiced. If an audit shows an overcharge of more than five percent (5%), Supplier shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

SECTION 4. FEES AND INVOICING
4.1 Buyer shall pay all undisputed amounts within 90 days after the date of Supplier's invoice. Invoices shall be issued monthly in arrears unless an SOW states otherwise.
4.2 Amounts not paid when due bear interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month and the highest rate permitted by law, from the due date until paid.
4.3 Buyer may withhold any amount it disputes in good faith by notifying Supplier of the dispute within 120 days of the invoice date, with reasonable detail of the reasons. The parties shall work together to resolve any dispute promptly.

SECTION 5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW on written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing it.
5.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately on written notice if the other party becomes insolvent, makes a general assignment for the benefit of creditors, or has a receiver appointed for its business.
5.3 Buyer may terminate any SOW for convenience on 15 days' written notice, paying for Services performed up to the effective date of termination.

SECTION 6. CONFIDENTIALITY
6.1 Each party (the "Recipient") shall keep confidential all non-public information disclosed by the other party (the "Discloser") that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential ("Confidential Information"), and shall use it only to perform or receive the benefit of this Agreement.
6.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to its Personnel and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less protective than this Section.
6.3 These obligations continue for two (2) years after the expiration or termination of this Agreement, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.
6.4 Within 30 days after a written request, the Recipient shall return or destroy the Discloser's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or as required by law.

SECTION 7. NOTICES
7.1 Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by email with confirmation of receipt, to the addresses set out on the signature page or as later updated by notice.
7.2 A notice is effective on receipt, or if delivered by courier, 2 Business Day(s) after dispatch.

SECTION 8. WARRANTIES
8.1 Supplier warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that each deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications for 45 days after acceptance.
8.2 Buyer's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in this Section is for Supplier to re-perform the non-conforming Services or, if Supplier cannot do so within a reasonable time, a refund of the fees paid for them.
8.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 9. EFFECT OF TERMINATION
9.1 On termination, Buyer shall pay all fees for Services performed through the effective date of termination, and each party shall return the other's Confidential Information as provided in this Agreement.
9.2 At Buyer's request, Supplier shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to 15 days after termination, at the rates then in effect.

SECTION 10. TAXES
10.1 Fees exclude sales, use, value added and similar taxes. Buyer shall pay any such taxes that Supplier is required to collect, other than taxes on Supplier's net income, property or employees.
10.2 If Buyer is required by law to withhold tax from a payment, it shall pay the amount withheld to the relevant authority and provide Supplier with evidence of payment within 120 days.

SECTION 11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
11.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

SECTION 12. DISPUTE ESCALATION
12.1 The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute through their project managers. If unresolved within 20 days, either party may escalate it to an executive of each party, who shall meet within a further 10 days.
12.2 Nothing in this Section prevents a party from seeking urgent injunctive relief.

SECTION 13. DATA PROTECTION
13.1 To the extent Supplier processes personal data on behalf of Buyer, it shall do so only on Buyer's documented instructions and in accordance with the data processing terms in Schedule D.
13.2 Supplier shall notify Buyer without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a security incident affecting Buyer's personal data.
13.3 Supplier shall retain Buyer's data for no longer than 180 days after the end of the applicable SOW, after which it shall securely delete that data unless retention is required by law.

SECTION 14. SEVERABILITY AND WAIVER

SECTION 15. FORCE MAJEURE
