RECITALS. Company wishes to obtain, and Contractor wishes to provide, the services described in this Agreement on the terms set out below.

SECTION 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 "Affiliate" means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party, where "control" means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting interests of the entity.
1.2 "Personnel" means the employees, agents and permitted subcontractors of a party who perform obligations under this Agreement.
1.3 "Documentation" means the user guides, specifications and operating manuals that Contractor makes generally available for the Services, as updated from time to time.
1.4 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which national banks in Dallas are authorized to close.

SECTION 2. EXPENSES
2.1 Company shall reimburse Contractor for reasonable travel and out-of-pocket expenses that Company has approved in writing in advance, at cost and without mark-up, provided they comply with Company's travel policy as provided to Contractor.
2.2 Expense claims must be submitted within 20 days after the expense is incurred, with receipts for any single item above two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

SECTION 3. ACCEPTANCE
3.1 Company shall test each deliverable against the acceptance criteria in the applicable SOW within 20 Business Days of delivery and notify Contractor in writing of acceptance or of any non-conformity.
3.2 If Company reports a non-conformity, Contractor shall correct it and redeliver within 20 days, and the acceptance procedure shall be repeated. If a deliverable fails acceptance three (3) times, Company may terminate the applicable SOW and recover the fees paid for that deliverable.

SECTION 4. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
4.1 The parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, agency or employment relationship between them.

SECTION 5. VENUE
5.1 Each party irrevocably submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Hennepin County, Minnesota, for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement, and waives any objection to venue in those courts.

SECTION 6. WARRANTIES
6.1 Contractor warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards, and that each deliverable will conform in all material respects to its specifications for 120 days after acceptance.
6.2 Company's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranty in this Section is for Contractor to re-perform the non-conforming Services or, if Contractor cannot do so within a reasonable time, a refund of the fees paid for them.
6.3 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY STATED IN THIS AGREEMENT, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY OTHER WARRANTY, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

SECTION 7. CHANGE CONTROL
7.1 Either party may request a change to the scope, schedule or fees of an SOW by written change request. Contractor shall respond within 10 Business Days with an estimate of the impact on schedule and fees.

SECTION 12. NOTICES
