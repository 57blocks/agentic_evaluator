MANAGED SECURITY SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Managed Security Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of September 2, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between Ironleaf Security Group LLC, a Virginia limited liability company ("Provider"), and Marlow Street Bank, N.A., a national banking association ("Customer").

1. SERVICES. Provider shall deliver 24x7 security monitoring, incident triage and quarterly penetration testing as described in Schedule A.

2. FEES. Customer shall pay a monthly fee of $64,000, invoiced in advance.

3. SERVICE LEVELS. Provider shall acknowledge critical alerts within fifteen (15) minutes. Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for missed service levels.

4. TERM. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for eighteen (18) months. It does not renew automatically. The parties may extend it only by a written amendment signed by both.

5. DATA PROTECTION. Provider shall process Customer data only on Customer's instructions and shall notify Customer of any security incident affecting Customer data within twenty-four (24) hours.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY.
6.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages.
6.2 Each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim; provided, however, that in no event shall either party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceed two million dollars ($2,000,000).
6.3 Notwithstanding Section 6.2, Provider's liability for breach of Section 5 shall not exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000).

7. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

8. REGULATORY ACCESS. Provider shall give Customer's regulators access to records relating to the Services on request.
