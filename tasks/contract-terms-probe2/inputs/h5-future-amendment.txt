SUPPLY AND DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Supply and Distribution Agreement (the "Agreement") is effective as of November 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date") between Tidewater Medical Supply, Inc., a Florida corporation ("Supplier"), and Brookhaven Clinics Group, P.A., a Florida professional association ("Customer").

1. SUPPLY. Supplier shall supply the medical consumables listed in Exhibit 1 against Customer's purchase orders.
2. PRICES. Prices in Exhibit 1 are fixed for the initial term.
3. DELIVERY. Supplier shall deliver within five (5) business days of each purchase order.
4. TERM. The initial term is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date. This Agreement then renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
5. LIABILITY. Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one million five hundred thousand dollars ($1,500,000).
6. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida.
