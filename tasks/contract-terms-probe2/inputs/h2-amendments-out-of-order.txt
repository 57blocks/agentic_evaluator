MASTER LOGISTICS AGREEMENT

This Master Logistics Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of May 16, 2022 (the "Effective Date") between Corvane Freight Systems, Inc., an Illinois corporation ("Carrier"), and Delmont Retail Holdings, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Shipper").

1. SERVICES. Carrier shall provide truckload and less-than-truckload transportation for Shipper's goods under tenders issued by Shipper.
2. RATES. Rates are set out in Appendix A and may be revised by written agreement.
3. CLAIMS. Carrier is liable for loss or damage to goods in transit as provided in Appendix B.
4. TERM.
4.1 The initial term is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.
4.2 This Agreement thereafter renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY.
10.1 Neither party is liable for lost profits or consequential damages.
10.2 Except for cargo claims under Appendix B, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one million dollars ($1,000,000).
15. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

Executed by both parties on May 16, 2022.
CORVANE FREIGHT SYSTEMS, INC.  By: /s/ Paul Deverell, President
DELMONT RETAIL HOLDINGS, LLC  By: /s/ Sharon Ito, VP Supply Chain
