MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Master Supply Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into effective as of September 1, 2023 (the "Effective Date") by Cobalt Ridge Manufacturing, Inc., an Ohio corporation ("Supplier"), and Fernhill Home Goods, Inc., a Georgia corporation ("Buyer").

1. SUPPLY. Supplier shall manufacture and deliver the products listed in Schedule 1 under purchase orders issued by Buyer.

2. PRICING. Prices are fixed for the first twelve (12) months and may then be adjusted once per year by no more than four percent (4%).

3. QUALITY. Products shall conform to the specifications in Schedule 2. Buyer may reject non-conforming products within thirty (30) days of delivery.

4. TERM. This Agreement has an initial term of five (5) years from the Effective Date and thereafter renews automatically for successive two (2) year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least one hundred eighty (180) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. LIABILITY. Supplier's total liability for any claim arising out of this Agreement shall not exceed the amounts paid by Buyer to Supplier under this Agreement in the twenty-four (24) months before the claim.

6. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.

7. AMENDMENTS. This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
