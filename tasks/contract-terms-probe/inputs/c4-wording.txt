PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT is dated as of the 15th day of January, 2025, and is effective as of February 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), between Brightwater Logistics Group, Inc., a Texas corporation (the "Company"), and Meridian Ledger Partners LLP, an Illinois limited liability partnership (the "Firm").

ARTICLE 1 — SERVICES
The Firm shall provide accounting systems advisory services to the Company as requested from time to time in writing.

ARTICLE 2 — COMPENSATION
The Company shall pay the Firm at the hourly rates in Schedule A, invoiced monthly and due within forty-five (45) days.

ARTICLE 3 — TERM
3.1 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of three (3) years.
3.2 The parties may renew this Agreement for one or more additional periods of one (1) year each by mutual written agreement executed no later than sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term. Absent such agreement, this Agreement expires at the end of the then-current term.

ARTICLE 4 — LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 In no event shall either party be liable for lost profits or consequential damages.
4.2 The Firm's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the greater of (a) two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000) and (b) the total fees paid to the Firm under this Agreement during the six (6) months immediately preceding the claim.
4.3 The limitation in Section 4.2 does not apply to (a) a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, (b) breach of Article 6 (Confidentiality), or (c) the Firm's indemnification obligations, for which the Firm's liability shall not exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000).

ARTICLE 5 — INSURANCE
The Firm shall maintain professional liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per claim.

ARTICLE 6 — CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care.

ARTICLE 7 — MISCELLANEOUS
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
7.2 Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement on January 15, 2025.
