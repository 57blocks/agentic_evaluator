CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement is made on June 1, 2025 between Tessellate Design Co., a California corporation ("Consultant"), and Orchard Row Health Partners, P.C., a Massachusetts professional corporation ("Client").

1. ENGAGEMENT. Client engages Consultant to redesign Client's patient intake forms and appointment flow as described in Exhibit A.

2. COMPENSATION. Client shall pay Consultant a fixed fee of $48,000, half on signature and half on acceptance of the final deliverables.

3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR. Consultant is an independent contractor and is responsible for its own taxes and insurance.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND PATIENT DATA. Consultant shall not access protected health information. Any information Client discloses is confidential and may be used only for the engagement.

5. OWNERSHIP. On payment in full, Client owns all deliverables. Consultant may show non-confidential images of the work in its portfolio.

6. TERM. This Agreement continues until the Services are completed, unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate this Agreement on fifteen (15) days' written notice. Client shall pay for work performed up to the termination date.

8. WARRANTY. Consultant warrants that the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner.

12. DISPUTES. The parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation for thirty (30) days. If unresolved, either party may pursue any remedy available to it.

13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, with Exhibit A, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject.
