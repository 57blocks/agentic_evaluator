MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2024 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and Larkspur Foods LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Customer").

1. SERVICES. Provider shall perform the data analytics services described in each Statement of Work executed by the parties (each, a "SOW"). Each SOW is governed by this Agreement.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT. Customer shall pay the fees set out in each SOW within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Late amounts bear interest at one percent (1%) per month.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY. Each party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence for five (5) years after disclosure and use it only to perform this Agreement.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY. Deliverables become Customer's property upon full payment. Provider retains its pre-existing tools and know-how.

8. TERM AND RENEWAL.
8.1 Term. The initial term of this Agreement is thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.
8.2 Renewal. This Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

9. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure within that period.

11. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY.
11.1 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental or consequential damages.
11.3 Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

14. GENERAL.
14.2 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.
14.3 Notices. Notices must be in writing and delivered to the addresses on the signature page.
