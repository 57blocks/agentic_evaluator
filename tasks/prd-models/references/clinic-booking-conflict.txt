# PRD: Clinic online booking

## Problem and goal
Patients should book appointments online without calling.

## User stories
- As a patient, I want to book a free slot online, so that I don't have to call.
- As a receptionist, I want to approve bookings, so that the calendar stays correct.

## Requirements
- PRD-1: Patients see the doctor's free 30-minute slots and book one.
  - Acceptance: a booked slot is no longer shown as free.
- PRD-2: The receptionist approves or rejects each booking.
  - Acceptance: a booking is valid only after approval.
- PRD-3: The patient is told the outcome.
  - Acceptance: the patient gets an instant "request received" message and a confirmation when approved.

## Out of scope
- Payments, multiple doctors, cancellations.

## Open questions / assumptions
- Conflict: the brief requires receptionist approval and also an instant confirmation. Assumed "instant" means an instant acknowledgement of the request, with the real confirmation after approval. Needs product sign-off.
