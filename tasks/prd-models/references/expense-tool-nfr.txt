# PRD: Internal expense tool

## Problem and goal
About 500 employees need to claim expenses, managers approve them, finance exports them.

## User stories
- As an employee, I want to submit an expense with a receipt photo, so that I get reimbursed.
- As a manager, I want to approve my direct reports' expenses, so that spending is controlled.

## Requirements
- PRD-1: Employees submit an expense with amount, category, date and a receipt photo.
  - Acceptance: submission without any of the four is rejected.
- PRD-2: The direct manager approves or rejects it.
  - Acceptance: the expense shows the decision to the employee.
- PRD-3: A manager sees only expenses from their own direct reports.
  - Acceptance: a manager requesting another team's expense is denied.
- PRD-4: Finance exports all approved expenses for a month as a spreadsheet.
  - Acceptance: the export contains every approved expense dated in that month and nothing else.
- PRD-5: Receipts are kept for 7 years.
  - Acceptance: a receipt cannot be deleted before 7 years from submission.
- PRD-6: Supports about 500 employees.
  - Acceptance: works with 500 users and their monthly volume without degradation.

## Out of scope
- Reimbursement payment, multi-level approval, currency conversion.

## Open questions / assumptions
- Where the org chart (who reports to whom) comes from is not stated.
