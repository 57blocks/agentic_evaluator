# PRD: Chat notifications

## Problem and goal
Users miss messages that matter to them.

## User stories
- As a member, I want to be notified when I am mentioned, so that I don't miss what needs me.

## Requirements
- PRD-1: Notify a user when they are @mentioned in a channel they belong to.
  - Acceptance: a mention produces one in-app notification for that user, and none for others.
- PRD-2: A user can turn notifications off per channel.
  - Acceptance: with notifications off, mentions in that channel produce no notification.

## Out of scope
- Email and push notifications; digests.

## Open questions / assumptions
- "Messages they care about" is ambiguous. Assumed it means @mentions; keywords, replies or whole channels are open questions.
- Delivery channel (in-app, email, push) is unspecified; assumed in-app only.
