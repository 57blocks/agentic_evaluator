# PRD: Recipe sharing site (v1)

## Problem and goal
Let people publish recipes and let anyone find them.

## User stories
- As a signed-in user, I want to publish a recipe, so that others can cook it.
- As a visitor, I want to search recipes by ingredient, so that I can cook with what I have.

## Requirements
- PRD-1: A signed-in user can publish a recipe with title, ingredient list, step-by-step instructions and one photo.
  - Acceptance: publishing without a title or ingredients is rejected; a second photo cannot be attached.
- PRD-2: Anyone, signed in or not, can browse recipes.
  - Acceptance: a signed-out visitor can open the recipe list and any recipe.
- PRD-3: Anyone can search recipes by title or ingredient.
  - Acceptance: searching an ingredient returns every recipe listing it.

## Out of scope
- Comments, ratings, following other users, and any other social feature.

## Open questions / assumptions
- Whether authors can edit or delete recipes is not stated; assumed out of v1.
