# Beneficial owner answers

Who the beneficial owners and fictitious beneficial owners are is already
graded by checks/ubo-check.mjs. Judge only the `reasoning` and `basis` text.

## correct-basis

Does each stated ground follow § 3 GwG as the Transparenzregister applies it?

- A share above 25% of capital or of voting rights; exactly 25% is not enough.
- Holdings through a controlled entity (more than 50% of its votes) count in
  full and are added to the person's own; they are never multiplied.
- Several controlled entities are added together; a 50/50 holding controls
  nothing.
- A company's own shares are left out of the base.
- The controller of a KG's general partner qualifies through it.
- The fallback names the Geschäftsführer, never a Prokurist.
